- `ScalingGroup.call` retries the gateway's HTTP 503 while a scale-to-zero group scales up from zero, and while the gateway is still connecting to a newly ready replica. It retries with backoff for up to `cold_start_timeout` seconds (default 300; `0` disables) and stops early if the group becomes `Failed`, `Error`, or `Unhealthy`. 503s returned by your application are not retried.
